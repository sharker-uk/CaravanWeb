Park Overview
Ground & pitches

The park starts overgrown. Click a tree or brush, walk up to it, then press E to clear it; trees yield saleable timber. Drag a box to place pitches; undersized boxes are shown in red.

Accommodation

Select an empty pitch, then place a unit.

Park facilities

Toilets are essential for every booking; tent and towed guests also require operational shared showers. Click a facility to inspect capacity and running costs.

Utility connections

Requires the shovel. Utility lines only work when they connect continuously to the incoming mains at the park boundary.

Management overlays

Tool Shop

Buy tools to unlock work abilities. In this prototype, tools unlock actions; you do not need to equip them or manage durability.

Shovel unlocks: gravel hardstanding pitches and utility trenching for water, gas, electricity and waste lines.

More tools can be added later for advanced landscaping, construction and maintenance.
